Privacy

This page explains what ExKnowledge collects, why, how long we keep it and how you use your rights. Last updated 28 September 2026.

Who is responsible

ExKnowledge is run by Andreas Parr Bjørnsund and his company in Norway, who are the data controller for this site. Privacy questions and requests go through the form at the bottom of this page.

Who processes data for us

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) forwards form submissions to our email inbox, which is hosted by Google (Gmail).
  • Google stores quiz and survey answers (Google Sheets) and, only with your consent, runs Google Analytics, Tag Manager and Google Ads.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site and counts visits without cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the files.

Some of these providers are based in the United States and may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, the transfer relies on the safeguards the provider offers, such as the EU standard contractual clauses. We never sell or rent your data.

Cookies

On your first visit a banner asks whether you accept analytics and advertising cookies. Choosing Reject keeps them off. To change your choice, clear this site's cookies and stored data in your browser, and the banner appears again. One small cookie remembers for 24 hours which language version you were sent to.

Your rights

  • See the data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Have it corrected or deleted.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it.
  • Withdraw your consent or unsubscribe at any time.
  • Complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, at datatilsynet.no.

We answer requests within 30 days.

Contact us about privacy

Use this form for any privacy question or request. We reply to the email address you give.
