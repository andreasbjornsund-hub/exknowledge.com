Privacy

ExKnowledge is a free reference site. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to reach us about it.

When you send a form

Newsletter sign-ups, questions, feedback, partner requests, PDF and glossary downloads are sent through FormSubmit and delivered as an email to the ExKnowledge editors. We receive what you typed, the page you sent it from and the time.

We use it to answer you, to send the file or update you asked for, and for occasional related ExKnowledge updates. We never sell or rent your details.

Analytics and advertising

With your consent, the site uses Google Analytics and Google Ads to count visits and measure campaigns, and Cloudflare Web Analytics for privacy-friendly page counts. You choose in the cookie banner on your first visit. Choosing Reject keeps analytics and advertising cookies off.

Your choices

  • Ask us to delete your details, or to stop sending updates, with the feedback form.
  • Change your cookie choice at any time by clearing this site's cookies in your browser.

Hazardous Area Guide network

ExKnowledge is part of the Hazardous Area Guide network of sites. The network's full privacy policy is at hazardousareaguide.com/privacy.
